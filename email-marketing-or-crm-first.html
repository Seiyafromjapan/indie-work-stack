Software decision guide

Should a solo business start with email marketing or a CRM?

The two categories work together, but they solve different problems. Buying both immediately is not always the best first step.

Start with a CRM when lead follow-up is the problem

A CRM helps keep track of enquiries, proposals, follow-up dates, and client context. Choose this path if your most common question is: “Who should I contact next?”

Start with email marketing when staying visible is the problem

Email marketing is useful when people know about you but are not ready to buy. A useful newsletter or short sequence can help you stay in contact until timing changes.

Start with neither when the offer is unclear

If you are still deciding what service to sell, whom to serve, or how prospects contact you, begin with a simple manual workflow. Software becomes easier to select after the process is visible.
