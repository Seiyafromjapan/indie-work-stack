Software decision guide

CRM features that matter for a solo service business

A CRM is useful only when it makes client work easier to manage. Start with a short requirements list—not a long comparison chart.

Published and fact-checked September 4, 2026 · By Seiya Utsumi

Five essentials

1. A clear contact record

See who the prospect is, what they need, how they found you, and the last meaningful interaction.

2. A simple pipeline

Use stages that match the way you actually sell: new enquiry, discovery call, proposal sent, follow-up, won, and lost.

3. A next-action reminder

A visible next action with a date is often more valuable than complex automation you never configure.

4. Searchable notes and history

Keep enough context to prepare before the next conversation.

5. A reasonable export path

Before subscribing, check how you can export contacts and core records if you leave.

Trial checklist

  • Can I add a new enquiry in under one minute?
  • Can I see every open opportunity and its next action?
  • Can I adapt the pipeline to my actual process?
  • Can I export core data?
  • Does the cost still make sense as my business grows?
