About

Clearer software decisions for independent work.

Indie Work Stack is for freelancers, consultants, designers, coaches, and other solo operators who want useful tools without an unnecessarily complicated stack.

Editorial approach

Every guide starts with a reader problem, not a commission rate. We explain when a tool category is useful, what a buyer should compare, who may not need it yet, and the limits of available evidence.

We verify factual claims against relevant official sources before publication. Sponsored relationships and affiliate links, if introduced, will be disclosed clearly near the relevant recommendation.
