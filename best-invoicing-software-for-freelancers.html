Best Invoicing Software for Freelancers: A Practical Shortlist

The best invoicing tool is the least complicated system that reliably creates invoices, records payment status, and gives you the reports your business needs.

Published September 10, 2026 · By Seiya Utsumi

Disclosure: This research-based guide contains no affiliate links at publication. Prices and features can change; verify them on the provider's website before buying.

The short answer

Start with a template if you invoice only a few clients and can track status consistently. Consider Zoho Invoice or Wave when cost is the main constraint. Consider FreshBooks when invoicing must connect with time, expenses, estimates, payments, and accounting. Check availability and payment support in your country before deciding.

What to compare

CriterionWhy it matters
Client and invoice limitsA low entry price may cover only a small number of billable clients.

Data exportYou need a usable exit route if pricing or requirements change.

A four-option shortlist

Invoice template

Best for occasional invoices and maximum control. The cost is manual numbering, status tracking, reminders, and reporting. Use a consistent file name and a separate invoice register.

Zoho Invoice

Worth evaluating when you want a dedicated invoicing product without starting with a paid accounting suite. Confirm current regional availability, payment integrations, and automation limits.

Wave

Worth evaluating for a small service business that wants invoicing and basic bookkeeping together. Check which products and payment services are available in your country and whether the current free tier covers your workflow.

FreshBooks

Worth evaluating when client billing needs to connect with estimates, time, expenses, payments, and reports. Its plans use billable-client limits, so compare the normal recurring price rather than a temporary promotion.

Choose by workflow

  • One or two occasional invoices: begin with a template and invoice register.
  • Cost-sensitive, invoice-first workflow: test Zoho Invoice and Wave.
  • Hourly or expense-heavy services: test FreshBooks' end-to-end flow.
  • Country-specific tax requirements: prioritize local compliance and accountant access.

Run one complete test

  1. Create a test client and service.
  2. Build an invoice with your real tax and payment terms.
  3. Preview the client's email and payment experience.
  4. Record a partial or full payment.
  5. Export the invoice and transaction data.
  6. Check the standard price and the cost at your expected client count.
