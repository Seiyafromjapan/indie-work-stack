What the result means

If the handoff works reliably with your calendar, notes, and invoicing setup, keeping that stack may be the right move. If a check fails because information is entered inconsistently, fix the procedure first. If the same changes repeatedly have to be copied across people and tools, a dedicated system may be worth trialing against this exact scenario. Client count alone does not settle the decision.
