Readiness review

- Which missing items block layout decisions?
- Which pages can proceed with approved content?
- Which pages need copywriting, client review, or a revised date?
- Who owns the next content decision?
- Where will all feedback be collected?
- Does any new request change the agreed scope?

Client handoff note

Before design or build work begins, please confirm which content is approved,
which content is still in draft, and who owns each missing item. This helps keep
the project moving without redesigning pages after final content arrives.

This is a workflow aid, not contract language or legal advice.
