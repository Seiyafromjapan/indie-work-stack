Choose by the next deliverable

Pick one measurable outcome. Check the current syllabus, workload, billing, cancellation, and refund terms before enrolling. Details and promotions can change.

A seven-day evaluation

  1. Write one 30-day business outcome.
  2. Find syllabus modules tied to it.
  3. Check the hours against your calendar.
  4. Read current payment terms.
  5. Choose one portfolio artifact.
  6. Complete a meaningful lesson.
  7. Continue only if it changes what you publish, measure, or sell.
