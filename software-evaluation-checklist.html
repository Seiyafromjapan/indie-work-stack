Software decision guide

Software evaluation checklist for freelancers

A free trial is useful only when you know what to test. Define the job first, complete one real workflow, and examine the cost of keeping or leaving the tool.

Published and fact-checked September 6, 2026 · By Seiya Utsumi

Evaluate five things

Check the job, workflow, full cost, data portability, and trial result. Reject a tool that cannot complete the essential workflow reliably, even when its feature list looks impressive.

1. Define one job

Describe the outcome in one sentence: “Remember every sales follow-up,” “publish a weekly email,” or “send proposals and collect approval.” A broad goal such as “organize the business” is too vague to test.

2. Write the essential workflow

List the trigger, the required steps, the information to record, and the final result. Separate requirements from preferences. A requirement must be present for the workflow to succeed; a preference makes the experience nicer.

3. Calculate the useful cost

Check the price at your expected usage, which limits cause an upgrade, and whether the needed automation or integrations require a higher plan. Include setup, migration, and weekly maintenance time in the decision.

4. Plan your exit before entering

Confirm what can be exported, in which formats, and what would need to be rebuilt. A tool that holds important contacts or content should have a practical way to recover or move that data.

5. Test one complete scenario

  1. Create representative sample data without using sensitive client information.
  2. Complete the workflow from its starting trigger to its final result.
  3. Test the most important integration or export.
  4. Record confusing steps and required workarounds.
  5. Repeat the workflow on another day before deciding.

Use a decision date

Set a date to choose Keep, Reject, or Test longer. If you keep the tool, schedule another review before a renewal or expected price increase. This prevents an unfinished trial from becoming a subscription by default.
